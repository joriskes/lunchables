- Every time someone figures something out with Claude, that knowledge currently lives on their machine
- A `CLAUDE.md` committed to a repo means the whole team benefits — not just the person who wrote it

Worth doing:

- Add a `CLAUDE.md` to every repo we actively work in
- Create a shared internal repo with global skills and a base `CLAUDE.md` — so nobody has to figure out dockerized, php-cs-fixer hooks, or legacy-api quirks from scratch
